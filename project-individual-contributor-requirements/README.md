## Individual contributor requirements

If you are completing the Capstone with a team, you will be evaluated both as a team and as an individual contributor.

> **It is possible for your team to pass the project while you do not pass individually.**

### Project contribution

> ✅ Any item marked incomplete in this section may require an individual redo.

- It is apparent from GitHub, Trello, project artifacts, and instructor check-ins that you were a meaningful contributor.
- You take ownership of assigned backlog items and follow them through to completion or communicate when they are blocked.
- You do not artificially inflate your contribution through unnecessary commits, trivial tasks, or misleading project records.
- Your commit messages are descriptive of the work completed.
- You contribute across the areas that are appropriate to your assigned role and project scope.
- You can explain the code, design, research, or project decisions you personally contributed to.

### Professional collaboration

- You communicate blockers and changes with your teammates and the instructional team.
- You participate constructively in planning, feedback, testing, documentation, and handoff/launch work appropriate to your role.
- You respect team decisions, client/stakeholder needs, and agreed project scope.

### Presentation

> ✅ Presentation participation is required.

- You contribute to the Capstone presentation.
- You demonstrate or discuss work that you personally contributed to during the project.
- You can answer reasonable questions about your contribution.

## Individual contributor evaluation

Your instructors will use the above requirements, GitHub history, Trello activity, project artifacts, presentations, standups, and one-on-one check-ins as evidence of contribution.
