# Capstone Presentation Checklist

## Timing

- [ ] Presentation is approximately 8-10 minutes.
- [ ] Team is prepared for 2-3 minutes of questions.
- [ ] Live demo is rehearsed.
- [ ] Backup screenshots or short recording are ready in case the live demo fails.

## Opening slide

### Client Track

- [ ] GA logo.
- [ ] Client logo.
- [ ] Project name.
- [ ] Team member names.

### Startup Track

- [ ] GA logo.
- [ ] Startup logo.
- [ ] Startup/project name.
- [ ] Team member names.

## Presentation story

- [ ] Problem/client/user is clear.
- [ ] Discovery/research is summarized.
- [ ] Proposed solution and MVP are explained.
- [ ] Development/planning process is shown.
- [ ] Product is demonstrated.
- [ ] Feedback and iteration are discussed.
- [ ] Technical highlights are explained.
- [ ] Challenges and lessons learned are included.
- [ ] Next steps are included.

## Team participation

- [ ] Every team member speaks.
- [ ] Every team member discusses or demonstrates work they meaningfully contributed to.

## Closing slide

- [ ] Uses the same required GA + client/startup branding as the opening slide.
- [ ] Includes a clear thank-you / questions prompt.
